# Heliopolis-AI — Regulatory source and scope register
Reviewed: 10 October 2026

This records which published rule a screening statement represents. It is not a legal opinion or engineering design.

## Solar ATAP — current non-domestic programme

Official SEDA sources:
- Programme technical studies and fees: https://www.seda.gov.my/reportal/atap/
- Eligibility, maximum demand, ten-year tenure: https://www.seda.gov.my/reportal/atap/solaratap-general/
- Guideline currently listed as 30 December 2025: https://www.seda.gov.my/reportal/atap/solar-atap-guidelines/

For non-domestic consumers, the published inverter AC capacity ceiling is the lesser of 1,000 kW and 100% of verified Maximum Demand (MD), subject to other eligibility and utility assessment conditions.

Published non-domestic technical assessment fees (check class/version before reliance):

| Rating | Study fee reference |
| --- | --- |
| 1–72 kW | No CAS capacity trigger |
| >72–180 kW | RM1,000 |
| >180–425 kW | RM5,000 |
| >425–1,000 kW | RM8,000 |
| HV connection | PSS, RM15,000 published listing; verify voltage-specific conditions |

The excess-energy offset arrangement lasts 10 years. From year 11, no surplus-export offset or rollover is allowed. Annual PV yield alone cannot estimate such savings: a customer load profile, generation/export modeling and approved tariff components are necessary. Heliopolis only provides a CAPACITY-ONLY precheck; even an apparently in-range result is not eligibility or financial approval.

## Historical NEM 3.0 NOVA — not Solar ATAP

Official SEDA programme and CAS fee table: https://www.seda.gov.my/reportal/nem/
Programme offer dates: https://www.seda.gov.my/reportal/nem/abouts-nem-3-0/

The published 2021–2025 NOVA quota offer period ended 30 June 2025 or when quota was exhausted, whichever first. The software's NOVA lookup is a historical fee benchmark and illustrative tariff sensitivity, NOT confirmation that new NOVA applications are available in October 2026. Check directly with SEDA for any later extension or valid legacy entitlement.

Historical NOVA CAS for LOW / MEDIUM VOLTAGE: 1–72 kW no study; >72–180 RM1,000; >180–425 RM5,000; >425–1,000 RM8,000; >1,000–2,000 RM15,000; >2,000–5,000 RM20,000. High-voltage CAS fees depend on qualified consultants. An UNKNOWN voltage class must not trigger a fixed fee quotation or implicit 11/33 kV selection.

## LSS/PPA, SELCO and grid assessment

LSS utility offtake and customer self-consumption must NOT inherit NOVA/ATAP consumer offsets, study fees or programme eligibility. All modeled tariffs and cashflows are illustrative; there are no binding PPA or electricity-bill inputs, nor an independently validated solar yield. A modeled positive NPV is not a bankability finding.

Heliopolis' AC MW / upstream MVA percentage is a heuristic, not a TNB load-flow or thermal capacity rule. Without utility network conditions, power factor, protection settings and an interconnection study, do not assert reverse-power trips, BESS mandates, grid approval or transformer sizing.

## Malaysian land-record legal limits

JKPTG general administrative guidance:
- Transfer restrictions, caveats, bank charges and necessary consents: https://www.jkptg.gov.my/index.php/my/pemilik-tanah
- Malay Reserve enactments and state variations: https://www.jkptg.gov.my/my/panduan/senarai-undang-undang/akta-enakmen/enakmen-rizab-melayu

These are general administrative explanations, not a definitive legal rule for every proposed lease or solar land conversion. State procedures vary. Ownership fraction sums do not prove that 51% of owners can bind 100% of a lot. A land category of Pertanian requires professional, state-specific development and land-use review; do not automatically assert conversion to Perindustrian under NLC section 124. Likewise, caveats, charges and Malay Reserve status require title-specific review rather than an unconditional yes/no transaction decision.
