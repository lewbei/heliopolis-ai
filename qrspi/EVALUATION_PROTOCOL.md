# Heliopolis-AI — Falsifiable evaluation protocol (not a field benchmark)

**Status 10 October 2026:** Protocol drafted; synthetic regression fixtures exercise bounded behaviors. **No held-out field-data comparison has been conducted.** Do not quote the regression-test pass fraction as OCR accuracy.

## Actual claim under test

On legitimately accessible Malaysian title/SLD documents, a Gemini-perception + deterministic-validation workflow reduces **confidently incorrect preliminary clearances** and analyst correction time compared with equally resourced simpler workflows, while retaining usable coverage.

## Comparators

- **B0 — Gemini direct:** supply identical documents and a fixed extraction instruction; report explicit unknowns.
- **B1 — Gemini + simple rules:** same extracted fields, straightforward schema/unit-check spreadsheet or script; no graph path audit.
- **H — Heliopolis:** same OCR model/version/prompt budget plus deterministic land, topology and finance checks.

Do not attribute an advantage to H if its OCR gets a better model or more calls. Report API/token, time and human-review budgets. A more complex method may be slower or abstain more; compare at matched review capacity as well.

## Target labels (expert-authored; not generated by the model under test)

For each title: title/lot/state, area, sum of ownership shares, material caveats/charges/reserve/restriction notes and whether original text is legible. Do not ask AI to determine legal approval.

For each SLD: whether PV/inverter/protection/transformer/grid symbols exist and their physically connected edges, based on independently annotated drawings. Reserve grid-code approval as a separate professional determination.

For each finance example: manually calculated reference NPV/IRR from identical inputs plus current scheme applicability. Synthetic finance input consistency tests are not empirical yield validations.

## Metrics and non-negotiable reporting

1. Field-level exact match; numerical area tolerance stated in advance.
2. **False-clearance rate:** any instance declared pass when the independent reference marks a critical inconsistency.
3. Coverage vs abstention vs manual-review rate; never count \`NEEDS_REVIEW\` as a true negative.
4. Review minutes per document, including correcting missed/false findings.
5. Total OCR requests, tokens/cost, latency, and production failure reasons.

Split by source (synthetic versus real), document legibility, jurisdiction, and out-of-distribution drawing styles. Present denominator and confidence intervals where applicable.

## Cheapest kill test before a pilot

1. Withhold a malformed title with unreadable encumbrance text. The system must not issue approval or substitute an example.
2. Use a drawing with a disconnected anti-islanding relay and a direct inverter-to-grid path. The graph screen must flag it.
3. Use a multi-MW C&I site labeled Solar ATAP. The system must refuse to claim Solar ATAP eligibility.
4. Use a broken model/API endpoint. Uploaded-document processing must stop with a review/error status.

The above are **regression tests**, not evidence of natural-document generalization.

## Decision thresholds for the proposed 3–5-site pilot

Pilot is a **measurement exercise**, not a guaranteed product validation. Define with reviewers *before* inspection: tolerable critical false clearances, maximum average correction time, document coverage, and the minimum improvement over B1 needed to justify operational deployment. If data are inadequate, report **not enough evidence** rather than extrapolating.

**Sources of rule eligibility:** official SEDA and Energy Commission programme documents, accessed/versioned at execution time. An unverified 15% MW/MVA heuristic is not a TNB study or battery requirement.
